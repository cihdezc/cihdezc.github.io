Urban sustainability challenges — mobility, green infrastructure, climate adaptation — cannot be solved by algorithms alone. This project develops grassroots-participatory AI methods that bring together optimization, community knowledge, and nature-based solutions to support urban transformation. The work is grounded in direct collaboration with community organizations and takes multi-objective decision-making as its core methodological thread.

Funded by the Google Academic Research Award 2024 on Society-Centered AI.
