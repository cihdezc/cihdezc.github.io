Prizes and Awards
======

* Premio AFIRME-FUNAM, Fundación UNAM, 2025 (5th edition). First place, research work category.
  Awarded for the work "Hacia modelos crediticios justos, transparentes y adaptativos por medio de aprendizaje por refuerzo multiobjetivo", which develops credit decision models that are fair, transparent, and adaptive using multi-objective reinforcement learning.

* Google Academic Research Award, Google, 2024.
  Awarded for the project "Community weavers of artificial intelligence and nature-based solutions: a grassroots-participatory AI approach to urban transformations towards sustainability", which develops participatory AI methods for urban sustainability with and for local communities.

* Third place, doctoral thesis contest in artificial intelligence, Sociedad Mexicana de Inteligencia Artificial (SMIA), 2019.

* Premio Arturo Rosenblueth, CINVESTAV-IPN, 2018.
  Awarded for the best doctoral thesis in computer science at CINVESTAV-IPN.

* Premio Estatal de la Juventud Nayarit, Government of the State of Nayarit, 2018.
  Awarded in the academic achievement category.

* First place, master's thesis contest in artificial intelligence, Sociedad Mexicana de Inteligencia Artificial (SMIA), 2015.

Recognition and Appointments
======

* Track Chair, Evolutionary Multiobjective Optimization track, GECCO 2026.
  GECCO is a leading conference in genetic and evolutionary computation, organized by ACM SIGEVO.

* Associate Editor, Engineering Optimization, 2026–present.
  Engineering Optimization is a peer-reviewed international journal covering optimization methods and their applications in engineering.

* SNI Level 1, National System of Researchers (SNII), 2019–2030.
  The SNII is Mexico's national recognition system for research excellence, administered by CONAHCYT.

* Invited participant, Dagstuhl Seminar 16412 on Automated Algorithm Selection and Configuration, Germany, 2016.

* Representante de Tutores, AI area, Posgrado en Ciencia e Ingeniería de la Computación, UNAM, 2025–present.
  Representative of faculty advisors in the AI area of UNAM's graduate program in computer science.

* Coordinador, Licenciatura en Ciencia de Datos, IIMAS-UNAM, 2026–present.
  Academic coordinator of UNAM's undergraduate program in data science, hosted at the Institute for Applied Mathematics and Systems.

* Coordinador, Diplomado en IA Aplicada, DGTIC-UNAM, 2024–present.
  Academic coordinator of a national continuing education program in applied AI, offered through UNAM's General Directorate of Computing and Information Technologies.
